# KINETIC — Open Questions

The concept proposal is a working draft. These decisions require confirmation from the gym owner before related features are treated as final requirements.

## Business and membership
- [ ] What services, membership types, prices, payment methods, and membership terms are currently offered?
- [ ] Which renewal, cancellation, pause, refund, and payment-plan policies apply?
- [ ] Which membership and financial actions can members complete themselves versus request from staff?

## Registration and access
- [ ] What is the current registration and check-in process?
- [ ] Should check-in use QR, an app code, manual search, or a combination?
- [ ] What fallback should reception use when a member has no phone or connectivity?
- [ ] Which roles are required and what can each role view or change?

## Staff operations
- [ ] Which staff workflows are repetitive or difficult today?
- [ ] Is a reception waiting queue useful?
- [ ] Are staff tasks, handover, and maintenance reporting required?

## Classes and coaching
- [ ] Which classes and trainer services should appear online?
- [ ] What are the booking, cancellation, rescheduling, waitlist, and availability rules?
- [ ] Who creates, reviews, and maintains workout content?
- [ ] What information may trainers see about assigned members?

## Marketplace and growth
- [ ] Is the marketplace browse-only, enquiry/reservation-based, or enabled for online payment?
- [ ] Are pickup and delivery supported?
- [ ] Which features may non-members access, and which are free, paid, or member-only?
- [ ] How should guest visits and referral attribution work?
- [ ] Are rewards or loyalty programmes wanted? If so, what are the rules and limits?

## Communication and data
- [ ] Which notification types are useful, how often, and how can users control them?
- [ ] What personal or health-related information is truly necessary?
- [ ] Who may access that information, and how will consent and retention be handled?
- [ ] What reports and metrics does the owner need, and who may see financial information?

## Delivery and operations
- [ ] Which devices, connectivity assumptions, hosting, support, and maintenance arrangements are required?
- [ ] What are the budget, timeline, testing, launch, and post-launch support expectations?

## Scope board

For each proposed feature, the owner can choose: **Keep, Modify, Remove, Add, Prioritize, Simplify, or Needs discussion**. Record owner notes and phase decisions before implementation.
